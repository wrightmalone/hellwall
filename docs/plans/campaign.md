# The campaign: missions and map types

For planning the campaign before the story and art passes. Each mission is framed as:

- **Hook:** what it asks of you, and why that's interesting.
- **Complication:** what changes partway and forces a rethink.
- **Catharsis:** the moment the mission is built to deliver.

Every mission should have one idea you can say in a sentence, a different one from the missions around it. Every threat should be told before it comes; the review list's main campaign complaint was "you learn it by dying".

Tags:
- **[now]** can be built with what the game already has: map kinds, objectives (survive, close gates, population, loot ruins, slay), triggers (on a day or after a goal: a message, a gift, an announced raid), locked buildings and units.
- **[new: ...]** needs a mechanic that doesn't exist yet, named in the tag.

## The twelve we have, and what each should be

### 1. The First Night (Plains, Easy, 15 days) [now]
- **Hook:** a handful of families, four militia and a Keep. Learn to build a town that feeds itself.
- **Complication:** the first wave comes from the one side you haven't walled.
- **Catharsis:** your first wall holds.
- **As it is:** it teaches building but has no complication yet. The first wave should come from a side you can see coming but haven't finished; the briefing already tells you where.

### 2. Iron in the Hills (Highlands, Easy, 20 days) [now]
- **Hook:** stone and iron everywhere, room to build nowhere. Grow to forty souls and arm them.
- **Complication:** the size of the town draws notice. Forty Hounds come from the east once you reach forty people, told 45 seconds ahead.
- **Catharsis:** your first army meets something fast and wins.
- **As it is:** good as is. The raid is a real choice: grow now and face it, or build soldiers first.

### 3. The Gatekeepers (Plains, Normal, 30 days) [now]
- **Hook:** you have to leave the walls. Two Hellgates feed every wave; close them.
- **Complication:** every soldier you send out is one fewer on the wall, and the waves keep coming while the raid is away.
- **Catharsis:** the second gate falls, and the next wave arrives half the size.
- **As it is:** the idea is right. The bot can't close gates on forested maps, so this needs your playtest.

### 4. Drowned Country (Lakes, Normal, 30 days) [now]
- **Hook:** the water walls for you, if you find where the land narrows.
- **Complication:** on day 12, winged things cross the water from the south, and the lakes that protected you don't stop them.
- **Catharsis:** a moat and towers together: walkers stopped by the water, fliers shot down over it.
- **As it is:** good. It's the first mission where terrain is the answer.

### 5. The Causeway (Causeway, Normal, 40 days) [now]
- **Hook:** a town on a land bridge, four gaps in the ridges to hold.
- **Complication:** holding the gaps close in is cheap but cramped. The good ground lies between your walls and the far gaps, and so does the wilds' next pack.
- **Catharsis:** you push the wall out to the far gaps, and the whole bank behind is yours.
- **Missing:** a moment. A mid-mission gift of stone, "if you can wall the far gaps", would make pushing out the explicit choice.

### 6. The Pass (Highlands, Normal, 40 days) [now]
- **Hook:** everything in the mountains comes down one narrow way.
- **Complication:** Spitters on day 20, which shoot over walls. Towers that were safe behind the parapet aren't any more.
- **Catharsis:** the slaughter count. Four thousand is the goal, and you watch it climb.
- **As it is:** it overlaps with The Bridge. See map type C below: The Pass could become a long gorge with the horde strung out in it.

### 7. Two Fronts (Two Fronts, Normal, 45 days) [now]
- **Hook:** two walls to hold, east and west, and a rich country to the north behind an old wall.
- **Complication:** the north is where the iron and silver are, and it's packed with sleeping demons. Clearing it takes soldiers off both fronts, and the noise of fighting wakes more.
- **Catharsis:** Exorcists, paid for with silver from the north, holding a front on their own.
- **Missing:** a reason to go north that the player can't skip. Lock Exorcists behind silver, or give a goal, "loot the two ruins in the north".

### 8. Wildwood Watch (Wildwood, Normal, 45 days) [now]
- **Hook:** wood for a hundred walls, and no clear ground to see them coming.
- **Complication:** the woods are your walls, until your own woodsmen cut them away. Howlers come on day 25.
- **Catharsis:** you deliberately cut a gap where you want them to come, and build the killing ground there.
- **Missing:** the living-woods idea should be the whole point here. A briefing line: "every tree you fell is a way in; fell the right ones".

### 9. The Reliquary (Plains, Normal, 35 days) [now]
- **Hook:** go out and take back the old towns: three ruins held by Thralls.
- **Complication:** the dead guard them, and they don't sleep deeply. Each raid risks waking what's around it.
- **Catharsis:** the soldiers come home loaded, and you spend the loot on the walls just before the Convergence.
- **As it is:** good. It's the counterpart to The Gatekeepers: loot instead of destroy.

### 10. The Long Siege (Plains, Normal, 60 days) [now]
- **Hook:** the full sixty days, with everything.
- **Complication:** none of its own, which is the problem. It's the sandbox as a mission.
- **Catharsis:** surviving sixty days.
- **Proposed:** keep it as the capstone of act 2, but give it a twist. Say that halfway through, a second Keep's refugees arrive (a gift of colonists, as the campaign already gives gold) and you have to house them fast. Or replace it with map type F.

### 11. The Bridge (Crossing, Normal, 50 days) [now]
- **Hook:** one bridge, and everything in the east comes over it.
- **Complication:** you know exactly where they'll come, and so does every Spitter and Gargoyle. Fliers ignore the river. By the last days the bridge alone isn't the problem.
- **Catharsis:** the Convergence pours over the bridge into the killing ground you spent fifty days building. This is the TAB moment you described.
- **As it is:** the bot loses it because it walls its whole town, not the bridge. Needs your playtest for difficulty.

### 12. The Hellwall (Plains, Hard, 60 days) [now]
- **Hook:** the last line. Four Hellgates, a harder horde.
- **Complication:** you must close all four gates, which means leaving the wall, while the waves grow toward the Convergence.
- **Catharsis:** the last gate falls ("Now hold the wall"), then the Convergence.
- **Missing:** it's on Plains. The finale deserves its own ground: map type G.

## Map types

### What we have
- **Plains:** open ground, some lakes, rock and wood. The sandbox.
- **Lakes:** water makes the chokepoints.
- **Highlands:** ridges and passes. Stone-rich, room-poor.
- **Wildwood:** forest everywhere. Wood-rich, blind.
- **Causeway:** a river down the middle, the town on the bridge, four gaps.
- **Two Fronts:** two fronts, and a rich infested north.
- **Crossing:** one bridge; everything over it.

### New ones worth making

#### A. The Island (sea all round, one sandbar)
- **Hook:** the sea is your wall. Only one sandbar reaches the mainland, and only fliers cross the rest.
- **Complication:** the sandbar is exposed at low tide, twice a day. Hold it then, rest between.
- **Catharsis:** a Gargoyle swarm breaks on a sky full of Skyspire bolts while the tide is in.
- **Needs:** [now] for a permanent sandbar; [new: tiles that change on a timer] for tides.

#### B. The Frozen River
- **Hook:** a river guards the east. It's the easiest wall in the game.
- **Complication:** on day N the river freezes, and it becomes a road. You're told ten days ahead.
- **Catharsis:** the new wall along the ice is finished just as the first wave walks across.
- **Needs:** [new: a trigger that changes tiles] (water to ice, walkable). A small one: triggers already fire on a day.

#### C. The Gorge
- **Hook:** a long canyon with the town at its mouth. The horde comes the whole length of it, strung out.
- **Complication:** the canyon walls are rock, and your miners wear rock away. Every quarry at the mouth widens it.
- **Catharsis:** Bombards firing down a crowded corridor.
- **Needs:** [now] (a map shape like the others). It could become The Pass.

#### D. The Ruined City
- **Hook:** you start inside a walled town that fell years ago. The walls are broken and the houses are full of Thralls.
- **Complication:** every house you purge is a house you can live in, and every purge is noisy.
- **Catharsis:** the old walls, mended, holding the first wave.
- **Needs:** [new: buildings placed by the mission at the start, some damaged or possessed]. Hand-placed packs and gates already exist; this is the same for buildings.

#### E. The Sealed Valley
- **Hook:** nothing attacks you. A pass to the north is closed, and the valley is quiet and poor.
- **Complication:** the resources you need are past the pass, and opening it lets the waves in. You choose when.
- **Catharsis:** the moment you're ready and open it yourself.
- **Needs:** [new: an objective or trigger on reaching a place, and a gate the player opens]. Waves that only start after an event are a small change to the survival schedule.

#### F. The Refuge
- **Hook:** other towns are falling, and their people are walking to yours.
- **Complication:** refugees arrive from the edges in groups, told ahead, and the horde follows them in. Protect the road, and house them.
- **Catharsis:** the last group through the gate, the gate shut behind them.
- **Needs:** [new: arriving colonist groups that walk in and can be caught]. An escort mission, which the review list says TAB did badly (hero missions). Keep it short and generous, or skip it.

#### G. The Hellwall itself (the finale's own ground)
- **Hook:** the wall at the end of the world, the Hellgates on the other side of it.
- **Complication:** the gates are beyond the wall. Every raid to close one is a long march out and back.
- **Catharsis:** the last gate closing within sight of your own wall, then the Convergence with nowhere to come from but one side.
- **Needs:** [now] (a shape: a great wall across the map with gates in it, the Hellgates beyond).

## An arc

**Act 1: A town**
- **Missions:** The First Night; Iron in the Hills.
- **What you learn:** build and feed a town; arm it.
- **Geography:** open.

**Act 2: The land**
- **Missions:** The Gatekeepers; Drowned Country; The Causeway; The Pass or The Gorge; Wildwood Watch; The Reliquary.
- **What you learn:** each mission teaches that the ground is the answer: water, bridges, passes, forest. The Gatekeepers and The Reliquary teach leaving the walls.

**Act 3: The march**
- **Missions:** Two Fronts; The Frozen River, or The Ruined City; The Long Siege (with its twist), or The Refuge.
- **What changes:** the map turns against you. Two fronts, safe ground that stops being safe, a town to take back.

**Finale**
- **Missions:** The Bridge; The Hellwall on its own ground.
- **What it asks:** everything at once, from the direction you know it's coming.
